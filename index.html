weblander

A drone, a desert, and a long evening. Fly where you like and set down gently.

Mouse steers — the further from centre, the harder you tilt.

Left button full thrust  ·  Middle hover.

Keyboard: arrows steer, Z thrust, X hover

Gamepad: left stick steers, RT or A thrust, LT or X hover

Full thrust climbs, hover holds the height you are at, and nothing ever drops out of the sky. Hover keeps the lean inside 45°; full thrust opens it all the way round — hold the stick hard over and you will loop, and the streamer will draw it for you.

Flat battery? Hold hover anyway — the rotors autorotate and you come down slowly enough to walk away.

F toggles fullscreen. The pointer is captured while you fly — Esc releases it, a click takes it back.

Tilt your phone to steer.
