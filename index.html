Gamepad: left stick steers, RT thrusts and drops, A thrust, LT or X hover

Hover holds the craft inside 45° of lean and keeps its climb at any of them. The first five seconds pin your height.

F toggles fullscreen. The pointer is captured while you fly — Esc releases it, a click takes it back.
