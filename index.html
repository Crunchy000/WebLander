twilight hover

a paper phoenix, a desert, and a long evening

Motion access was refused, so the thumb stick is steering instead.

Fly where you like and set down gently. The phoenix starts small, with one tail feather.

  • Gather the five flames. Each burns under a column of light; fly through one and the bird grows a feather, a longer streamer and more room for energy.
  • Keep your energy up. Set down on level ground to charge, hold still over a paper tulip to sip its nectar, and fly low through the lamps on the water.
  • Stack tricks. Loop the loop, bounce off the top of a balloon, fly through the bunting, knock over a stack or a tree, skim low over the sea, shave past a tree, squash or flip a crab. Keep them coming and the multiplier climbs; set down to bank the stack — crash and it is lost. You can land on the canoes, too.
  • Tick off the awards. Twenty-seven of them, from your first loop to fifty crabs — see the awards page.
  • Mind the shadow crabs. When one rears up, climb. Come down on one to squash it, or drop fire and flip it on its back.

There is no running out of birds: the phoenix always rises again from its flame on the pad — but each death costs it the last flame it gathered, which burns again where it was found.
