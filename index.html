weblander

A paper bird, a desert, and a long evening. Fly where you like and set down gently.

There are folded paper tulips in the meadows. Hold still just above one and the bird drinks from it — nectar is charge, and one flower soon after another scores more each time.

Mouse steers — the further from centre, the harder you tilt.

Left button full thrust  ·  Middle hover.

Keyboard: arrows steer, Z thrust, X hover

Gamepad: the left stick steers: up flies away, down towards you, left/right tilts that way. The right stick is height and sidestep — left centred the bird holds its height, up climbs, down sinks, left/right steps sideways, level. RT or A full thrust, LT or X hover.

Full thrust climbs, hover holds the height you are at, and nothing ever drops out of the sky. Either way the lean goes all the way round — hold the stick hard over and you will loop, and the streamer will draw it for you.

Flat battery? Hold hover anyway — the rotors autorotate and you come down slowly enough to walk away.

F toggles fullscreen. The pointer is captured while you fly — Esc releases it, a click takes it back.

Sticks: a stick appears under each thumb. Left steers: up flies away, down towards you, left and right tilt that way. Right is height and sidestep: left alone the bird holds its height, drag up to climb, down to sink, sideways to step aside, level.

Tilt: lean the handset to steer. One finger anywhere on the screen hovers; two fingers is full power.
